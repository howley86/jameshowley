James Howley is a graphic designer based in Dublin. He provides graphic design and visual communications across a range of media for a variety of businesses, services and individuals. James adopts a conceptual and content-focused approach to his design work, with a strong emphasis on typography. James studied Design Communications at WIT before completing his degree with first class honours at Hereford College of Arts in the UK.

Since 2011, he has worked in both freelance and in-house roles, working closely with clients with the aim of creating distinctive and effective visual solutions. James has designed everything from brand identities and print publications to marketing and advertising campaigns, across sectors such as retail, hospitality, fashion, transportation, finance and marketing.
